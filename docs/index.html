Methodology

Kronos samples possible price paths from historical index bars. The median is the central forecast. Shaded bands show the spread of simulated closes at each step. All times use India Standard Time.

The scorecard compares each eligible forecast’s final median against the observed close at its target. The baseline carries the last observed price forward. Lower mean absolute error is better. Coverage measures how often an observed close fell inside the published band.
